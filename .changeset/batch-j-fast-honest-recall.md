Semantic search finds your own sessions again, the decision guard can say "not decided yet", and semantic builds and index rewrites take turns.

- **Your sessions first.** The semantic index now keeps subagent vectors apart from your own sessions, so a default search (subagents hidden) ranks only your sessions instead of losing them behind subagent matches. On a measured index where 58% of the vectors came from subagents, one of five test queries got no main-session semantic hits before and every query got its full 60 after, and the semantic half of a search went from about 275 ms to about 115 ms. Searches that include subagents are unchanged.
- **A one-time rebuild.** After the upgrade, the app (or `cal watch`) rebuilds the semantic index once in the background, about a minute after it has caught up with your sessions; `cal embed` does it before it starts. It needs no model download, holds the index for about 10 s on 324k vectors (writes elsewhere in the app wait meanwhile), and waits when the disk has less than about three times the semantic index free. Until it runs, search works as before. Restart a `cal watch` or `cal embed` that was left running from an older version: it can still search the rebuilt index, but it can no longer add vectors to it.
- **Less work per top-up.** Background top-ups run on 2 to 4 threads instead of 8 (about the same CPU per message, without keeping 8 cores busy), a text repeated within a build (a prompt sent to many subagents) goes through the model once, and harness boilerplate (the local-command caveat, interrupt markers, session-limit notices, slash commands without arguments) no longer gets a vector. Keyword search still finds all of them.
- **A guard that can say no.** `check_decision` and `cal check` keep every close match (a restatement or reversal of the proposal) and at most two related decisions, only from a calibrated 0.72 similarity and near the best one. With no close match they lead with "No decision on this yet.", and `check_decision` adds `noDecision: true` to its structured result (the `/recall` skill, now 1.3.1, explains it). Before, 96% of proposals checked in a project that never decided them came back with a list of 6 decisions on average; now 6.5% get a related one, labelled as context.
- **A quieter recall hook.** The proactive recall hook (`cal recall-now`) only speaks on a close match: on real prompts from another project it added a note to 22 of 60, now to 1.
- **Recall without the model.** Without the embedding model, `cal decisions`, `cal gotchas` and `cal check` print the closest past sessions by keyword (they printed nothing), and the MCP recall tools add them to their "semantic recall unavailable" notice. Plain `--json` output is unchanged.
- **Literal project filters.** `-p` / `project` fragments match `_` and `%` literally in recall, `check_decision`, search, todos and the recurring-issue fixes (`-p my_app` no longer matches `myXapp`).
- **Builds and rewrites take turns.** A semantic build now holds `embed.lock` next to the index: `cal embed` refuses (naming the holder) while the app or `cal watch` builds, and the app's builds skip while `cal embed` runs. Shrink index and Reclaim space refuse while `cal embed` builds and hold `rewrite.lock` for the whole rewrite, under which `cal index`, `cal watch`, `cal embed` and `cal distill --auto` refuse to start.
- **Live memory in the app.** An open Project page's decisions, gotchas and TODOs now refresh with the other live views as sessions are indexed, so memory an agent records while the app is open shows up without a reload.
